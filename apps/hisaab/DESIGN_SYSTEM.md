# Hisaab design system — "the passbook"

Money apps should feel calm and trustworthy, so Hisaab borrows from a bank passbook rather than a flashy dashboard.

- **Signature:** the passbook ledger: ruled rows, a stamped date in the margin, amounts right-aligned, and a running balance column when you pick one account (`LedgerRow`, `DateStamp`).
- **Colour:** paper `#F0F2EF`, ink `#12241F`, evergreen `#0B4F3C` (money you have, the header band), brass `#A8761F` (goals and warnings), rust `#B8432C` (money going out), green `#1D7F4E` (money coming in). Full dark mode.
- **Type:** Instrument Serif for the figures that matter (net worth, totals, goals); Figtree for everything you operate.
- **One bold element:** the evergreen band on Home with the net-worth number in large serif.
- **Shape:** 10 px corners, hairline borders, no shadows.
- **Privacy:** "Hide amounts" swaps every figure for ₹ ••••; the app locks with a PIN after 1 minute in the background.
