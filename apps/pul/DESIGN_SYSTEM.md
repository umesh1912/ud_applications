# Pul design system — "airmail"

Sending money abroad is like posting a letter overseas, and people worry about hidden costs. The design leans on airmail and on showing every rupee.

- **Signature:** the red–blue chevron border of an airmail envelope (`Airmail` in `widgets.dart`) frames anything about to cross a border: the quote, the review and the receipt. Status is a round, slightly tilted **postmark** (`Postmark`).
- **Colour:** cool paper `#F4F5F9`, ink `#141B2D`, airmail blue `#1F3F94`, airmail red `#D2352B`, delivered green `#1E8E5A`, amber `#B7790B` for demo and tax notes. Full dark mode.
- **Type:** Archivo Narrow (tall, condensed, like an envelope label) for headings and big amounts; Public Sans with tabular figures for everything else.
- **Honesty rules:** the price breakdown always lists our rate vs mid-market, our margin in ₹, the fee, GST and TCS before the Send button. A "demo" note sits on every money screen because rates are simulated.
